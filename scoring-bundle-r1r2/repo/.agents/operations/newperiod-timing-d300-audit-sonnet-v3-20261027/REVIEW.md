- **Atomic reservation (W2-A1 part 1).** `WaveLedger` (`<namespace>/wave-ledger.json`, one small JSON, `flock` exclusive): every slot records its last fresh own allocation (`observed`) and the allowance it reserved for its single active write (`reserved`). A new file is admitted only under the lock, after (1) outstanding = every other slot's observed+reserved, plus this slot's fresh observed plus the new allowance <= wave aggregate limit (9,252,122,208), and (2) when the shared fresh wave snapshot is older than 1 s the whole wave is re-measured under the same lock and (fresh + others' reservations + allowance) must also fit. `finish_active` releases the reservation and records the fresh own allocation. The former per-process 1 s cache is gone. Tests: 8 threads and 6 separate processes race for headroom for exactly 3 allowances: exactly 3 succeed (thread and cross-process flock tests); release returns headroom; the stale-cache counterexample (current aggregate above the row, cached snapshot inside its TTL) is rejected at the first admission by a measured (not skipped) fresh observation.
- **Invariant enforced during writes.** The monitor (task check every 0.25 s) now also verifies each worker's true allocation <= its ledger `observed + reserved + 4 MiB slack` (`RESERVATION` kill) and re-measures the whole wave every 1 s; so the ledger sum is a sound upper bound on the wave aggregate as long as each monitor lives. A worker killed mid-write leaves its reservation held: that fails closed for the rest of the wave (the wave has failed anyway; no retry).
